Symptom Tracking V1

What a lesson in product management. Drunk with my newfound Claude-enabled power, I packed too much into this next pass. I used it for about a month, interating over that time, but would call this app a failure.

Once again, I used Claude to develop a PWA that I could use on my phone. Though the code is too messy to be worth sharing, I did come away from the project with a stronger sense for what does work. Namely:

  • The simple, delightful logging interface, even for so many items; tapping icons on my phone > typing into an excel sheet.
  • Something like the macro score - my attempt to summarize the overall picture - is useful.

But it was too much:

  • Bloated with symptoms and new functionality, the app quickly became unwieldy. I'd re-start from scratch with a streamlined set of inputs + simpler functionality to ensure clean code.
  • I didn't need the complexity of the charting + scores on my phone. For multiple reasons, I'd prefer it live more cleanly on my desktop. Next time, I'll develop for that transition.
