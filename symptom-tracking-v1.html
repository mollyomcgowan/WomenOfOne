Symptom Tracking V1

What a lesson in product management. Drunk with my newfound Claude-enabled power, I packed too much into this next pass. I used it for about a month, interating over that time, but would call this app a failure.

Once again, I developed a PWA. The code is too messy to be worth sharing, but - before I critique it - here are some things I did like:

  • The simple, delightful logging interface works well for this; tapping icons feels better and was easier to keep up than typing data into an excel sheet
  • Thinking about how to summarize to a something like a macro score was valuable

But it was too much. Here are a few things I learned:

  • I didn't need the complexity of the charting + scores on my phone. Ultimately, I realized I didn't need that kind of analysis on mobile and would prefer it lived more cleanly on my desktop.
  • Starting so big meant a lot of bugs; next time, I'd re-start from scratch with simple functionality to ensure clean code.
